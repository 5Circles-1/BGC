Morning war room — 20 minutes, phones down

  • Min 0–5: Dashboard on screen. Yesterday's row read aloud: revenue, spend, CPL, sales.
  • Min 5–10: Every red or amber KPI — owning head states the fix, not the reason.
  • Min 10–15: Blockers board: anything open > 48h escalates to Management now.
  • Min 15–20: Each department commits to 3 outcomes for today. Written here, checked tomorrow.

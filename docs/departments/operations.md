# Operations — Department Playbook

**Mission:** deliver so well that students become the next marketing channel — webinars run flawless, onboarding lands inside 24 hours, and proof (testimonials, results, referrals) flows back to Marketing every week.
**The number Operations owns:** webinar show rate (base plan: 38%) and onboarding TAT (< 24h).
**Team:** ops lead + webinar producer (hire on trigger W3+) + support + trainers (Harsh for algo content; batch faculty as today).

## The webinar engine (E3's production line)

Cadence: 1 webinar (W2) → 2/week (W3–4) → alternate days (W5+) → evergreen replay slots (W6+). Every run uses the same checklist:

**Before**
- Registration page → instant WhatsApp confirmation + calendar invite (Make).
- Reminder sequence: **T-24h (value clip), T-2h (personal note), T-15min (join link)** — this sequence *is* the show rate; it gets its own QA.
- Tech check 60 min prior: stream, slides, checkout link, backup host device.
- Rahul Sir's run-of-show: 45 min teaching (real skill, no tips), 15 min offer, disclaimer slide in the deck (compliance G4).

**During**
- Moderator marks attendance live → `Lead_Log` flags Attended (Make S4 once upgraded, manual export until then).
- Engagement flags captured: poll answers, questions asked → closers see who leaned in.
- Checkout link + deadline stated once, clearly — scarcity must be true (batch start date).

**After**
- Replay to no-shows within 24h; attendee list to Sales **the same evening** — follow-up starts while the webinar is warm.
- Debrief in 15 min: show rate, drop-off minute, questions asked → one fix for the next run.

## Onboarding (< 24h, every student)

1. Welcome call same day: schedule, platform access (Rigi/community), what happens in week 1.
2. Community add + introduction post; batch WhatsApp group.
3. Expectations set in module 1: risk disclosure, no-tips policy, refund terms — trust built on Day 1 prevents refunds on Day 20.
4. Fee record verified against `Collections` (no access without a matching payment row — protects everyone).

## Retention & proof loop (the flywheel Marketing runs on)

- **NPS at Day 7 and Day 30**; target ≥ 8. Any cohort < 8 → founder session that week.
- Testimonial pipeline: every happy student asked once, on a consent form → **3 clips + 2 result screenshots to Marketing weekly** (no earnings claims, real names with permission).
- Referral engine (from W5): every new student asked at onboarding; reward paid the same week it converts (with Finance).
- Support inbox: **first response < 4h, 9 am – 9 pm**; unresolved > 24h escalates to ops lead.

## The offline engine (E2 keeps its floor)

- Batch calendar published for the full 60 days (TDP/TVP/Elite/CTC/Grow+/BIT as scheduled); batch fill % tracked weekly.
- Mid-batch upsell moment scripted (week 3 of any batch): next-level batch or Membership.
- Walk-ins from local ads handled same-day by floor counsellors; every walk-in enters `Lead_Log` like a digital lead — one funnel, one truth.
- Classroom proof content (with student consent) shot weekly for @_5_circles relaunch.

## Win-back & no-show flows

- No-show (webinar): replay + call in 48h → next webinar invite → Nurture.
- Lost deals (W6 win-back sprint): fresh offer + new proof, called by the closer who knows them.
- Dormant alumni: alumni-only webinar in W2 (E1 harvest) — upgrade path, referral ask.

## Interfaces

- **Gives Sales:** attendee lists + engagement flags same evening; walk-in leads logged.
- **Gives Marketing:** consented proof weekly; webinar dates 7 days ahead for ad scheduling.
- **Gets from Sales:** closed-won handoff ≤ 1h with what was promised, in writing.
- **Gets from Finance:** paid roster daily (access control); **Gets from HR:** producer/support staffed ahead of the cadence increase.

## Week-1 checklist

1. Webinar #1 dry run end-to-end (registration → reminders → room → checkout → attendance export).
2. Reminder sequence built and tested on the team's own numbers.
3. Onboarding kit ready: welcome-call script, access checklist, module-1 disclosure.
4. Consent/release form printed and in use; first 3 testimonial clips delivered.
5. Alumni webinar (E1) scheduled for W2; past-student register imported to `Lead_Log` (with Sales).

# HR — Department Playbook

**Mission:** staff the machine *ahead* of the curve — hiring fires on volume triggers, not on overwhelm — and keep the floor paid, scored and motivated for 60 hard days.
**The number HR owns:** role-fill TAT ≤ 7 days, attrition 0 during the sprint.
**Team:** Sanya (HR + payroll admin) + Management for offers.

## Hiring triggers (pre-approved — when the trigger fires, HR hires; no fresh debate)

| Trigger (2 consecutive days) | Hire | TAT | Base comp guide* |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sprint start (already fired) | 2 closers + 1 media buyer | 7 days | Closer ₹15–18k + incentive; buyer ₹25–35k |
| Hot leads > 25/closer/day | +1 closer | 7 days | as above |
| 2+ webinars/week sustained | +1 webinar producer/support | 7 days | ₹15–20k |
| 15+ creatives/month needed | +1 editor (or contract per-piece) | 5 days | ₹18–25k / per-piece |
| Support tickets > 20/day | +1 support/ops | 7 days | ₹12–15k |

*Calibrated against the current register (payroll ≈ ₹2.8L/month net, avg ₹16–19k). Closers' **on-target earnings must cross ₹35k** via incentives — in Kanpur that hires the best callers on the market and keeps them.

## The 7-day hire pipeline (per role)

Day 1: JD out (WhatsApp status, staff referral bonus ₹2,000, local job groups, Apna/WorkIndia post) → Day 2–3: phone screens (for closers: a 2-min mock pitch on the phone — hire the voice, train the script) → Day 4: floor interview + roleplay → Day 5: offer, references → Day 6–7: docs + join. Backup candidate always kept warm.

**Closer profile:** Hindi/Hinglish fluent, evenings + weekends OK (webinar follow-up hours), hungry for incentive pay, teachable > experienced. Ex-edtech/insurance callers are gold.

## Ramp (5 days to quota)

Day 1–2 shadow the best closer + product immersion (sit through a batch class and a webinar replay) → Day 3 scripts + objection roleplay, pass the compliance quiz (no-promises rules) → Day 4 supervised live dials → Day 5 solo with daily scorecard. Not at quota by Day 10 → one coaching week, then decide fast — a struggling closer burns leads, and leads cost ₹80 each.

## Pay, incentives, morale (attrition insurance)

- **Payroll by bank transfer, on date, every time** — via the salary register (advances capped so net pay ≥ 0 per the manual). The Dec-2025 ₹7.9L full-and-final wave is the cautionary tale: people leave when pay wobbles or targets feel rigged.
- Sales incentives (4–6% of verified collections) computed with Finance, **paid weekly** — nothing motivates like Friday credit.
- Leaderboard daily; small spot bonuses (best call of the week, first close of the day).
- Founder 10-minute 1:1 with every new joiner in week 1; HR pulse check biweekly.
- Sprint intensity is real ("day and night") — one guaranteed weekly off per person, rostered so coverage holds 7 days. Burnout in W5 costs more than a day off in W2.

## Scorecards (every role, one page)

Each role gets 3–5 numbers, reviewed daily-to-weekly: closers (dials, connects, closes, collections), media buyer (CPL, leads, ROAS), producer (show rate, TAT), support (response time, CSAT). The scorecard is the appraisal — no surprises at Day 60.

## Interfaces

- **Gives Sales:** closers on trigger, ramped; roster covering 9 am – 9 pm × 7.
- **Gives all departments:** onboarding, scorecards, incentive clarity.
- **Gets from Management:** pre-approved comp bands + budget per trigger (no per-hire negotiation delays).
- **Gets from department heads:** vacancy/overload flags 7 days ahead, in the war room.

## Week-1 checklist

1. Closer JD + media-buyer JD out Day 1; referral bonus announced to staff.
2. 2 closers + 1 media buyer offered by Day 7 (pipeline of 4+ candidates each).
3. Incentive plan signed off with Finance/Sales; comp bands documented.
4. Ramp kit ready: scripts, compliance quiz, shadow roster, scorecard templates.
5. 7-day coverage roster published (with weekly offs protected).

# Management — Department Playbook

**Mission:** own the number. Run the cadence, clear blockers inside 48 hours, release spend only into working rates, and protect the company from the five ways this target dies (`ROADMAP.md §10`).
**The number Management owns:** ₹ collected vs ₹25,00,000 — pace, every single day.
**Who:** Rahul Saraoge (founder) + directors. One person is named **Growth Owner** on Day 1 — the throat to choke for the sprint itself.

## Decision rights (set once, Day 1, then the machine runs)

| Decision | Owner | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Price book | Management | Locked Day 1; changes only at weekly review |
| Ad-spend tranche | Management + Finance | Weekly, released only while CPL ≤ plan and ROAS ≥ 3 |
| Hiring on trigger | HR (pre-approved) | No fresh debate when a trigger fires |
| Kill/scale an offer or campaign | Weekly review | Data from the tracker, not opinions |
| Compliance gates G0–G4 | Management | Blocking — nothing overrides them |
| Blockers > 48h | Management | Named owner + date, same war room |

## The cadence (chaired by the Growth Owner; portal on screen)

- **Daily 9:15 am — war room, 20 min** (agenda lives in the portal's War Room tab). Founder attends; absence is how cadences die.
- **Daily 9:00 pm — scorecard closes.** A missing number = a red flag with the same severity as a bad number.
- **Monday 10:00 am — weekly review, 60 min:** numbers vs plan (15') → each department 5' (KPI table, one win, one fix) → kill/scale decisions → next week's targets on the record → compliance + cash check.
- **Day 15 / 30 / 45 — checkpoints** with go/no-go criteria (`ROADMAP.md §6`). Day 30 below ₹8L = the recovery playbook *is* the plan; that decision is made in that meeting, not deferred.

## Protect the founder (the funnel's face is a finite resource)

Rahul Sir's sprint calendar, defended by Management: 2 webinars/week + 1 batch-shoot block (3h) + daily war room + closing support for the biggest deals only. Everything else is delegated. A second host is trained by W5 and the best webinar goes evergreen by W6 precisely so the target stops depending on one person's stamina.

## The don't-do list (scope discipline for 60 days)

1. **No new products mid-sprint.** The price book of Day 1 is the catalogue of Day 60.
2. **No off-book discounts** — bonuses instead (extra batch access, community months).
3. **No cash for online sales; no s.269ST breaches offline** (₹2L cash-receipt cap is mechanical).
4. **No advisory revenue, no "SEBI-Reg." claims, until the registration number exists.** Stride stays parked; the RA application runs in parallel, not in the funnel.
5. **No spending into a broken rate** — the recovery playbook freezes spend at the last profitable level.
6. **No skipped war rooms.** If the founder can miss it, everyone can.

## What Management reviews weekly (the five dials)

1. **Pace:** cumulative ₹ vs the week's cumulative target (portal header).
2. **Unit economics:** CPL, show, close, AOV vs the living scenario (Funnel tab).
3. **Cash integrity:** gateway ↔ books reconciliation, registers current, refunds < 5%.
4. **Capacity:** leads per closer, webinar seats, any trigger fired but unfilled.
5. **Risk:** compliance incidents (target: zero), ad-account health, blocker aging.

## Escalation matrix

| Signal | Response |
|---|---|
| KPI red 2 consecutive days | Owning head brings a fix to the next war room |
| Blocker > 48h | Management takes it, names owner + date |
| Week < 70% of target | Sunday emergency review; recovery playbook |
| Compliance violation | Same-day stop of the asset (ad/script), retrain, log |
| Checkpoint miss (D30/D45) | Scenario shift decided in the meeting |

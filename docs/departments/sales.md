# Sales — Department Playbook

**Mission:** convert attention into collected revenue — speed-to-lead under 5 minutes, a disposition on every call, a next step on every deal.
**The number Sales owns:** ₹ collected/day vs the daily requirement (portal shows it every morning).
**Team:** sales head + closers (2 hired Week 1, #3 on trigger) + floor counsellors for walk-ins.

## The July lesson, made into law

July generated 260+ Facebook leads. Every single one still sits at status "CREATED" — nobody logged a call, nobody dispositioned, nothing converted. That is the most expensive habit in the company and it ends now: **a lead without a disposition by end of day is a war-room escalation.** The backlog itself is Week-1 revenue: every July lead gets called with the webinar invite script — even a 5% revival at ₹15k is ₹2L.

## CRM v1 (stages live in `Lead_Log` + `Sales_Pipeline`)

The old CRM training doc was empty; this is the replacement, running on the Master Tracker until a dedicated CRM earns its place.

`New → Contacted → Qualified → Webinar Registered → Attended → Follow-up → Won / Lost / Nurture`

Rules:
- **Disposition after every call attempt** — no exceptions, 10 seconds in the sheet.
- Every open deal in `Sales_Pipeline` has a next action dated ≤ 72h out. No next action = it isn't a deal.
- `Lost` requires a reason (price / timing / trust / junk). Reasons feed Marketing daily.
- `Nurture` = real but not now → re-enters via the win-back sprint (W6).

## Speed-to-lead: ≤ 5 minutes

Make S1 pings the on-duty closer the second a lead lands, with name + phone + intent answers. Contact-rate decays by the minute; this SLA is checked against timestamps, not memory. Coverage roster: 9 am – 9 pm, seven days — "day and night" was the commitment; this is where it cashes in.

## Webinar follow-up SOP (where the ₹ actually is)

- **Attendees:** 100% called within 24h. Five touches over 7 days: call → WhatsApp recap + offer → founder voice note → student case study → deadline call. Then Nurture, not silence.
- **No-shows:** replay link + call within 48h → invited to next webinar.
- **Non-buyers at deadline:** Membership (₹999–₹1,999) as the down-sell — a paying community member today is next month's flagship student.

## Script spine (Hinglish; full scripts to be recorded and QA'd in Week 1)

1. **Open:** "Rahul Sir ke webinar/institute se…" — context in 10 seconds.
2. **Qualify:** goal (income/skill), experience, time available, who decides.
3. **Pitch:** ONE offer for this cohort + what changes for them in 90 days. Education outcomes — *skill, process, discipline* — **never profit promises**.
4. **Close:** genuine deadline (batch start date) + EMI option + next step on the call.

Objection sheet: "fees zyada" → EMI + value stack (not discounts — price book is law) · "market risky hai" → exactly why education first; we teach risk management, we don't sell tips · "sochke batata hoon" → book the decision call now · "spouse se poochna hai" → invite spouse to a 10-min call.

## Rules of the floor

- **No cash for online sales.** Gateway link on the call, payment confirmed before celebration. Offline counter follows the fee-register process.
- No promise a compliance check wouldn't survive — QA reviews 5 recorded calls/week; one violation = same-week retraining, second = off the phones.
- Payment confirmed → handoff to Operations within 1 hour (welcome call same day).

## Interfaces

- **Gives Finance:** payment confirmations same hour; deal data clean in `Sales_Pipeline`.
- **Gives Operations:** closed-won handoff ≤ 1h with expectations set (batch, dates, what was promised).
- **Gives Marketing:** daily lead-quality verdict + which angles produced buyers.
- **Gets from Marketing:** leads in real time with intent answers attached.
- **Gets from HR:** closers hired on the 25-hot-leads/day trigger, ramped in 5 days.
- **Gets from Operations:** attendee lists live during webinars, engagement flags (poll answers, questions asked).

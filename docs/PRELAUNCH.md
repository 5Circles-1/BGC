# The Launch Readiness Runway — T-15 to Day 1

**Prep window: Tue 11 Aug 2026 (T-15) → Tue 25 Aug 2026 (T-1). Sprint Day 1 = Wed 26 Aug 2026 (T-0). Day 60 = Sat 24 Oct 2026.**

Thirteen working days. Sat 15 Aug is Independence Day (banks and registrar shut — internal work only); Sundays 16 and 23 Aug are catch-up days with one reading task each.

The live version of this document is **OPERATOR module M0 — Launch Readiness** (`portal/index.html`), which is the dashboard's home screen until T-0. It carries 36 gates, 119 steps, 15 role charters, 15 tools and 8 offline tripwires, each checkable. This file is the narrative and the reasoning behind them.

## 1. The finding that changes the plan

**Roughly ₹10.25L of the ₹25L monthly revenue mix cannot lawfully or physically launch on Day 1 — and up to ₹14.6L if Scanner Lite also slips.**

| SKU | Plan/month | Day-1 status | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Course + P1b bump | ₹8.07L | **Ships** (partial) | Modules 1–3 in Hinglish, Modules 4–8 on a dated release calendar inside the 30-day doubt window |
| P4a City Workshop | ₹2.50L | **Ships** | Kanpur only, own classroom, 40 seats, one dated cohort |
| P2a/P2b Scanner Lite | ₹4.40L | **Conditional** | Depends on a contract engineer signed by T-13 and the T-5 go/no-go |
| P2c Scanner Pro | ₹3.00L | **Deferred** | No named registered analyst, no CO, no back-test, no forward record, algo question unanswered |
| P3 Research Subscription | ₹5.00L | **Deferred** | Same five blockers |
| P4b Mentorship | ₹2.25L | **Deferred** | Invite-only to P1 buyers who do not exist until ~Day 30; faculty load needs 8h/week of a founder who has 5 |

Five independent blockers sit on the research line, any one of which is sufficient on its own: **zero named SEBI-qualified registered analysts against two planned; no named Compliance Officer; no back-test evidence of any kind; no forward paper-trading record; and the algo-framework question unanswered.** Hiring a qualified analyst does not compress into fifteen days for a regulated role in Kanpur. That is a structural constraint, not an execution failure.

### What this does to the ₹25L target

The Day-1 sellable ceiling is **₹10.6L–₹15.0L/month** of the planned mix. To reach ₹25L by Day 60 the research line has to open by roughly **Day 20–25** and ramp inside the remaining weeks. So there are two honest positions, and the CEO picks one:

- **Hold Day 60.** Research must open by ~Day 20. That means the analyst is hired and the CO named inside sprint weeks 1–2, and the back-test plus 30 forward sessions complete on schedule. Probability of ₹25L drops below the ~25–30% already stated.
- **Re-base.** Day 60 targets ₹15–17L on the education line plus Scanner Lite, and ₹25L moves to Day 85–95 once research is genuinely open. This is the ~75% case and it is the one a regulated business should want.

**The plan and the runway are identical either way.** Only the number on the wall changes. Deciding this now, in writing, is worth more than deciding it at Day 45 when a floor of fifteen closers is behind target and someone starts improvising.

**The deferred SKUs are recorded as failed gates, not forgotten.** G-PRO is expected to fail at T-1 and is recorded as failed with evidence, so nobody later believes the SKU was overlooked rather than consciously withheld.

## 3. The critical path

Two chains determine whether Day 1 holds. Everything else has slack.

**Chain A — the registration chain (decides whether half the revenue exists):**
`T-15 pull the registration file → T-13 board resolutions naming PO and CO → T-9 the research go/defer decision → T-5 sign-off chain → research opens or is formally deferred`

The decision is forced on **T-9 (Mon 17 Aug)**, deliberately, not discovered at T-1. That gives Sales and Finance a full week to re-plan Desk B onto workshops, Scanner Lite annual and alumni upgrades rather than research.

**Chain B — the product chain (decides whether Scanner Lite exists):**
`T-15 open the data-vendor process → T-13 sign the engineer → T-12 licensed data validated by spot-check → T-9…T-6 build → T-5 Lite go/no-go → T-2 final price list`

**External lead times that fifteen days cannot compress.** Each needs a fallback decided in advance, not discovered at T-1: platform advertiser verification, WhatsApp API with DLT registration, telephony vendor KYC, market-data licensing, and the second gateway settlement rail. The marketing fallback is explicit — if no paid channel is verified by T-2, Day 1 runs on organic, the warm base and the offline engine, and **launch is not delayed for it**.

## 5. The cannibalisation question, answered

The ₹1,999 online course and the ₹15,000 TDP classroom batch sell to overlapping people. Ignoring that is how a sprint quietly eats its own base.

**They are different products for different jobs.** P1 is recorded, national, self-serve, and teaches concepts. TDP is live, Kanpur, classroom, mentored, with a teacher who answers you by name. The ₹13,000 difference buys presence, correction and accountability — and that is exactly how it must be said, on every call, in the Academy Day-1 module, and in the counsellor briefing.

**Three mechanical rules (gate G-O3):**
1. Kanpur-area leads showing walk-in intent route to floor counsellors, not to Desk A. One funnel, one truth, but the right desk.
2. No closer may discount TDP to match P1. Bonuses replace discounts; the price book is law.
3. P1 is a **feeder**, instrumented deliberately: P1 buyers in the Kanpur catchment are a workshop and TDP upsell list, tracked as such rather than hoped for.

**Tripwire:** if the TDP enquiry-to-admission rate drops against baseline while P1 sales rise, cannibalisation is real, not theoretical — enforce the routing rule and re-brief the floor that week.

## 6. Protecting the offline business

The ~₹5.9L/month offline engine pays today's salaries. It is the floor under the sprint, and the sprint is the thing most likely to damage it.

- **Baselines are measured at T-9, before anything changes** (G-O1): batch fill %, walk-in response time, monthly fee income, student NPS, faculty load. A tripwire with no baseline cannot trip.
- **The ringfence is a signed roster** (G-O2) naming the people who may not be pulled onto sprint work, plus immovable founder calendar blocks protecting batch teaching. Everyone named is told in person.
- **Eight tripwires with pre-agreed automatic pull-back actions** — decided now, in the calm, so they are not re-debated at Day 40. The strongest one: if offline fee income falls below 90% of baseline for two consecutive weeks, the founder returns to his full teaching load and the sprint's claim on his calendar shrinks to the war room and shoot blocks. **Offline recovery outranks the sprint number.**
- **Scheduling that costs nothing:** the biggest shoot block runs on Independence Day, when classrooms are closed anyway — zero batch revenue lost, only the founder's holiday.

## 7. The go/no-go

**T-1 (Tue 25 Aug), 18:00.** All eight domain owners plus retained counsel. Gate states are read off M0, not asserted from memory.

Two rules make the board real:
- **The verifier is never the doer.** Counsel, who did none of the operational work, independently tests G-A through G-F against their written pass tests that morning. A gate cannot be marked passed in M0 without a named verifier.
- **A waiver is a decision, not a shortcut.** Only the Compliance Officer may waive, with written reasoning recorded against the gate. "Waived" is never a synonym for "we ran out of time."

Any blocking gate not passed and not formally waived means **that scope does not launch** — not that the date slips for everything. The runway is designed so a single failure removes one SKU or one channel, not the sprint.

**T-0 (Wed 26 Aug), 08:30** — live-fire check before the first dial: one real test purchase completes end to end. **09:00** first huddle. **09:30** the floor dials.

## 8. Is fifteen days enough?

**For the education line: yes, but only with the two engagements in §2.** Without the Compliance & Documentation Executive and contracted recruitment, Sanya is the critical path for compliance evidence, hiring and money rails simultaneously, and one of the three will be late.

**For the research line: no, and no amount of effort changes that.** A registered analyst cannot be sourced, offered and joined in thirteen working days, and a defensible back-test with out-of-sample results is a multi-week job that produces a worthless answer if rushed. The runway builds the *durable* parts — the protocol with published thresholds, the licensed point-in-time data, the pre-registered rule, and a forward paper log that starts at T-13 so the 30-session clock is nearly complete by Day 15 — and opens the out-of-sample block once, after Day 1, with the analyst present and a witness.

**The minimum defensible Day-1 launch, if only 60% of this lands:** P1 (Modules 1–3 with a dated calendar) + P1b + P0 drip + P4a Kanpur workshop, sold by Desk A on the phone, with Desk B working the alumni base for workshops. No research, no mentorship, no Scanner Pro. That is a real business with honest products, and it is a far better Day 1 than a full price list the company cannot deliver.

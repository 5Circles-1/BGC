# Compliance Guardrails — 5 Circles Private Limited

**Owner: Management + Finance. Status: gate G0 must be cleared before the first rupee of ad spend.**

Compliance is not a brake on the 60-day target — it is what keeps the engine running at Day 30 when volumes are high. An ad account ban or a SEBI notice in Week 4 kills the target outright. Clear these gates once, early, and the rest of the plan runs without interruption.

---

## G0 — The registration gate (Day 1–2, blocks advisory revenue only)

India splits our two revenue lines very differently:

| Line | Regulator position | Can we sell it online now? |
|---|---|---|
| **Education** (courses, workshops, webinars teaching concepts, no buy/sell calls) | No SEBI registration required | **Yes — start Day 1** |
| **Advisory** (stock tips, buy/sell calls, model portfolios, "premium calls" groups) | Requires SEBI registration — Research Analyst (RA Regulations 2014) or Investment Adviser (IA Regulations 2013) | **Only if registered** |

**Action:** Management confirms in writing on Day 1 which registration 5 Circles holds (RA number `INH…` / IA number `INA…`), in whose name, and its validity.

- **If registered:** the number appears on the website footer, every landing page, every invoice, and ad landing pages. Proceed with both revenue lines.
- **If not registered:** the 60-day plan runs on **education revenue only** (this plan's math already works on education alone). File the RA application in parallel (NISM XV certification + application via RAASB); advisory revenue switches on the day the certificate arrives. **No tips, no calls, no "guaranteed profit" groups in the meantime — including free Telegram/WhatsApp calls, which SEBI treats as advisory activity.**

## G1 — Advertising code (Week 1, before scaling spend)

- SEBI-registered IAs/RAs must get advertisements **pre-approved** (BASL for IAs, RAASB route for RAs) under the SEBI Advertisement Code. Approval takes days, not hours — submit the first ad batch in Week 1 so approvals never block the spend ramp.
- Every ad and landing page carries the standard market risk disclaimer: *"Investment in securities market are subject to market risks. Read all the related documents carefully before investing."* and *"Registration granted by SEBI … does not guarantee performance or provide any assurance of returns to investors."* (registered entities).
- **Banned claims — automatic rejection of any creative containing them:** guaranteed/assured returns, "double your money", specific profit percentages promised, screenshots of P&L presented as typical results, "risk-free". Testimonials must be genuine, consented, and never imply typical profits.
- No paid association with unregistered finfluencers (SEBI's 2024 restrictions on regulated entities associating with unregistered persons giving securities advice).

## G2 — Meta platform verification (Day 1–5, blocks all paid social)

Meta requires **advertiser verification for financial products & services ads targeting India** (business verification + confirmation of regulator registration for securities-related ads). Unverified accounts get ads rejected or the account restricted — mid-campaign.

- Complete Business Manager verification for the 5 Circles legal entity now, not when the first rejection happens.
- Add the ad account payment method (currently missing — see roadmap Day 1 checklist) with the company card, not a personal card.
- Run all ads from the company Page with the SEBI number (if registered) in the Page bio.

## G3 — Money handling (Week 1)

- All online collections through a payment gateway (Razorpay / Cashfree class) settling into the **company** current account — no personal UPI for business fees. Every payment auto-generates a GST invoice (18% on services) numbered per the fee register series in `5C_Fees_and_Registers`.
- Published refund policy on the website (what's refundable, within how many days). Refunds processed within the stated window — refund disputes are the #1 source of gateway holds at scale.
- EMI/BNPL options only through the gateway's compliant providers.

## G4 — Data & communication (Week 1–2)

- Lead forms state what the number/email will be used for; WhatsApp marketing only via WhatsApp Business API with opt-out honored immediately (DPDP Act consent basics).
- Call recordings (sales QA) disclosed to the caller.
- Student data lives in the CRM and master tracker only — no personal phones holding exported lead lists.

## Standing rules for every department

1. **Marketing** never publishes a creative that isn't on the approved-claims list. New angle → 24h compliance check by Management before spend.
2. **Sales** scripts contain no return promises. QA samples 5 recorded calls/week for claim violations. One violation = script retraining same week.
3. **Finance** reconciles gateway settlements to invoices weekly; GST filed on time.
4. **Operations** keeps the disclaimer slide in every webinar deck and course module 1.
5. **Management** owns this document; review every Monday in the weekly review for the first 60 days.

# 5 Circles — 60-Day Growth Roadmap to ₹25,00,000

**Sprint window: 11 August → 9 October 2026 (60 days).**
**Target: ₹25,00,000 collected revenue across the window — measured as money received, not bookings.**

This roadmap was built from what actually exists today: the audit report, the accounts manual, the fee registers, the July Facebook lead exports, the Instagram audit, the connected Meta ad account and the Make.com workspace. Every number below is either taken from those sources or clearly marked as a planning assumption to be replaced by real data in Week 1.

---

## 1. The honest frame

5 Circles already collects **≈ ₹5.9L/month** in fee income (₹71.4L across FY25-26, cash + bank). Held steady, the existing offline machine contributes **≈ ₹10–12L** of the ₹25L on its own. The sprint's real job is therefore:

1. **Keep the offline base running** (₹10L floor, Operations + Sales), and
2. **Build a measurable online engine that adds ₹13–15L in 60 days** (Marketing + Sales), and
3. **Make every rupee visible daily** (Finance + the tracking system), so corrections happen in days, not months.

That is aggressive — roughly doubling the run-rate — but it is arithmetic, not hope. The infrastructure claim ("we have everything ready") is *mostly* true; Section 2 lists the five things that are genuinely not ready and get fixed in the first 48 hours.

## 2. Where we actually start (verified 10 Aug 2026)

**Working for us**
- Founder brand: Rahul Saraoge, 20+ years, 10,000+ trained, 4.9★ JustDial, **@rahul_saraoge ≈ 15K Instagram followers** — the single biggest marketing asset.
- Offline machine in place: classrooms, batches (TDP/TVP/Elite/CTC/Grow+/BIT), 15–18 staff, fee income flowing.

**Not ready — fixed in the first 48 hours**
1. The connected Meta ad account has **no payment method** and **no Facebook Page linked** → cannot run a single ad today.
2. **260+ July leads were never worked** — every row still sits at status "CREATED". Lead generation without follow-up is money burned.
3. **No price book exists.** Ticket sizes are undocumented (only signals: TDP ₹15,000; Membership ₹1,000), so targets can't be set per closer until prices are locked.
4. **CRM discipline is a stub** — the training doc is empty; no stages, no SLAs, no dispositions. The tracking system in this repo replaces it (see `docs/TRACKING-SYSTEM.md`).
5. **Make.com is on the Free plan** (1,000 ops/month, 2 scenarios) — dies within days at planned lead volume. Upgrade before launch.

**Hard gate before any advisory revenue**
Draft marketing copy claims "SEBI-Reg. Analyst", but no registration is evidenced in any document. Until a real registration number exists: no advisory sales (Stride stays parked), no tips/calls groups, and every "SEBI-Reg." claim comes out of bios and creatives. The entire ₹25L plan below runs on **education revenue only**, so this gate costs the target nothing — it protects it. Details and all other gates: `docs/COMPLIANCE.md`.

## 6. Checkpoints (Management owns these)

- **Day 15:** Real CPL, show rate, close rate vs the table above. Decide which scenario we are living in; re-forecast; adjust spend tranche.
- **Day 30:** Cumulative ≥ ₹8L or the recovery playbook becomes the plan. Also: hiring triggers review, refund rate < 5%, cash controls actually running (registers populated, weekly banking happening).
- **Day 45:** Cumulative ≥ ₹15L → green-light W7 harvest spend. Below → shift W7–8 fully to pipeline harvest + warm base, cut cold spend.

## 7. Recovery playbook (when a week lands < 70%)

Diagnose in this order — fix the *first* broken rate, not all four:

1. **CPL > 1.5× plan** → creative problem or audience fatigue: new hooks from testimonials, revert to the verified-form format that produced serious respondents in July, tighten targeting. Do **not** raise budgets to buy through a bad CPL.
2. **Show rate < 30%** → reminder problem: T-24h / T-2h / T-15min WhatsApp sequence, calendar invite on registration, founder voice-note reminder. Move webinar to 7–9 pm slot.
3. **Close < 2.5%** → offer or follow-up problem: listen to 5 recorded calls same day; check speed-to-lead log; simplify the pitch to one offer + one deadline; add EMI. If attendees are junk, fix form screening first.
4. **AOV < ₹15k** → discounting leak: enforce the price book; replace discounts with bonuses (extra batch access, community months).

One rule while recovering: **spend freezes at the last profitable level; it never scales into a broken rate.**

## 10. Risks that can kill the target, and their guards

1. **Compliance breach** (fake SEBI claim, banned ad promise) → account ban / SEBI action. Guard: gates G0–G4, claims checklist, one approver. — `docs/COMPLIANCE.md`
2. **Leads generated, not called** — already happened in July. Guard: speed-to-lead SLA + Make S1 timestamps + it's the first number read in every war room.
3. **Cash leakage at scale** — audit found ₹33.7L cash fees with ₹47k banked and multiple unexplained gaps. Guard: all online revenue via gateway only; offline per Accounts Manual (weekly banking, ₹25k float, no cash salaries); s.269ST ₹2L cash-receipt cap respected mechanically. Growth capital must not enter a leaky bucket.
4. **Founder bandwidth** — Rahul Sir is the funnel's face. Guard: batch-record creatives (one 3-hour shoot = 2 weeks of ads), evergreen webinar by W6, second host trained by W5.
5. **Ad account fragility** — new account, no spend history. Guard: complete business verification, warm up spend gradually (₹3–5k → scale ≤ 30%/day), claims hygiene, backup Page admin + 2FA.

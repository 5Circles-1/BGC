# Sales Playbook — 5 Circles

Phone-first, Hindi/English, tier-1/2/3 India. **No webinars for acquisition** — live sessions exist only for high-ticket conversion (invite-only, existing buyers) and retention (weekly subscriber session).

## The ladder (prices are locked — no off-book discounts)

| Tier | Product | Price (incl. GST) | Sold by | Class |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | 7-Day Market Basics (WhatsApp/Telegram) | Free | Automated | Education |
| P1 | Market Foundation Course (8–10h recorded, lifetime, 30-day doubt group) | **₹1,999** | Desk A, one call | Education |
| P1b | Sector Playbook Pack (checkout bump, ~25% take) | +₹499 | Automated | Education |
| P2a | Scanner Lite monthly — user-defined conditions, **no recommendations** | ₹499/mo | Desk A attach | Education/SaaS |
| P2b | Scanner Lite annual | ₹4,999/yr | Desk A + B | Education/SaaS |
| P2c | Scanner Pro — entry/SL/target signals | ₹14,999/yr | **Desk B only, after KYC** | **Research (RA)** |
| P3 | Research Subscription — positional calls + rationale | ₹24,999/yr (₹7,999/qtr) | Desk B | **Research (RA)** |
| P4a | City Workshop (one day, offline) | ₹9,999 | Desk B | Education |
| P4b | Mentorship Cohort (12 weeks) | ₹74,999 | Desk B senior only, invite-only to P1 buyers | Education |

Why ₹1,999: below the ~₹2,000 UPI impulse threshold an Indian retail buyer decides alone on one call; above it they consult family and the call dies. Blended P1 AOV with bump ≈ ₹2,124.

## Two desks

- **Desk A — Acquisition.** Fresh leads → P1 + Scanner Lite attach. Scripted, 12–18 minutes, 100 dials/day ramped (70 in month 1), 3.2 P1 sales/day ramped. One promise, one price, zero refund ambiguity.
- **Desk B — Value.** Buyers aged **30–90 days** → P3, P2c, workshops, mentorship. Consultative, 60 dials/day, ≈₹3.5L/month per ramped rep. Desk B is half the revenue target and sells only to people who already paid once. **Never point Desk B at cold traffic.**

## The single-call P1 structure (12–18 min)

1. **Open (60s):** context from their form answer; permission to proceed. No "sir, investment opportunity".
2. **Discovery (3–4 min):** capital band (indirectly), experience, one burn story, goal. Qualify honestly — wrong buyers refund.
3. **Teach one thing (2–3 min):** one real, useful concept. Credibility is the pitch.
4. **Offer (2 min):** what P1 is, what it is not (not tips, not advisory), price, lifetime access, doubt group.
5. **Close (2 min):** one price, one link, silence after the price. UPI on the call.
6. **Attach (1–2 min, after payment):** Scanner Lite as the practice tool. Never before the P1 payment completes.
7. **Disposition (60s):** CRM stage + next step date, always.

## Objection bank (the core; rebuttals stay compliant)

1. "Sochke batata hoon" → respect + one question: what specifically needs thinking? Book a slot, keep the door open.
2. "Paise nahi hai" → the course costs less than one bad trade; no EMI drama at ₹1,999; offer P0 nurture if genuine.
3. "Free me sab YouTube pe hai" → agree; structure and sequence are what they're buying; doubt group is the difference.
4. "Guarantee kya hai?" → we guarantee the teaching, never returns — anyone who guarantees returns is breaking the law; that honesty is the trust close.
5. "Wife/husband se poochna hai" → below-threshold price exists for this; offer to send a one-line WhatsApp summary they can share.
6. "Trading se kamai ho jayegi?" → honest answer: most people lose money without process; we teach process; markets carry risk.
7. "Tips do na course nahi" → tips without registration are illegal and tips without understanding are gambling; that's exactly why the course exists. (Desk B may later offer the registered research service — after KYC.)
8. "Pehle scam hua hai" → acknowledge the industry's reputation; SEBI registration number, named analysts, recorded calls — verify us first, buy second.

## Compensation

- Desk A: ₹18,000 fixed + ₹250/P1 + ₹400/Scanner-annual → OTE ≈ ₹30,000 at 83 P1 + 25 scanner.
- Desk B: ₹28,000 fixed + 4% of P3/P4 collections.
- **Clawback:** incentive on any sale refunded within 30 days reverses. Non-negotiable — it makes mis-selling unprofitable.

## Discount authority

None on the floor. The price book is the price. "Discount" requests are answered with bonuses (extra doubt-group months, workshop seat priority) approved by the Sales Head — never a lower price. TLs carry 40% personal quota and cannot approve exceptions either.

## The never-say list (breach = same-week retraining; second = escalation)

Guaranteed/assured returns · double/triple your money · sure-shot/jackpot calls · "SEBI approved profits" · loss-if-you-don't-buy urgency · fake scarcity · advisory language on education products · any specific return percentage, ever.

Speed-to-lead SLA: **first dial within 5 minutes** of lead drop. 7-touch/14-day cadence; day-21 recycle. Every call recorded and disclosed; QA samples weekly.

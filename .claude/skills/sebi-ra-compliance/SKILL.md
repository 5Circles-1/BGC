# SEBI RA Compliance — 5 Circles Private Limited

Registration: SEBI RA **INH000020004** (verify status in writing with the Compliance Officer before relying on it in any output). These rules encode the firm's operating policy under the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 and related circulars **as understood at Aug 2026 — always confirm against current SEBI circulars and the Compliance Officer; this file is policy, not legal advice.**

## 1. The fee cap — per family, not per invoice

- **₹1,51,000 per family per annum** for individual and HUF clients (non-accredited), effective 8 Jan 2025, CII-indexed, reviewed 3-yearly. Applies to existing and new clients.
- Counts **research-service fees only** (Scanner Pro, Research Subscription), measured **ex-GST** — statutory levies sit outside the cap. Education products (course, workshops, mentorship, Scanner Lite) sit outside.
- The check runs **before a payment link is generated**, on a rolling-12-month window per family code. Over-cap → block, no override below the Compliance Officer. OPERATOR module M9 is the ledger of record.

## 2. Advance fees and refunds

- Advance fees: **maximum 12 months**. Two-year and lifetime research plans are not sellable.
- Premature termination: refund the **pro-rata unexpired period**. **Breakage fees are prohibited — ₹0, always.**
- Education-product refunds follow the published policy (not the pro-rata rule).
- Incentive clawback on refunds within 30 days is firm policy (anti-mis-selling), not a SEBI rule.

## 3. Education is not research — the bright line

> **If the user sets the condition, it is a tool. If we set the condition and tell them to act on it, it is research.**

- "RELIANCE crossed the 200-DMA filter you saved" = tool (Scanner Lite, education brand).
- "BUY RELIANCE, SL 1420, TGT 1580" = research recommendation (Scanner Pro / Research Subscription, RA entity).
- Research services require: signed client agreement, KYC, risk profiling **before access**; fee-cap accounting; full RA disclosures. Education and research run on **separate brands, landing pages, ad accounts and books** (client-level segregation between research and distribution also applies).
- Free Telegram/WhatsApp "calls" groups are research activity too — never run them as marketing.

## 4. Advertisement Code (RAs, effective 1 May 2023)

**Automatic fail — never publish:**
- Return/performance claims: "double your money", "sure-shot", "guaranteed", "risk-free", "confirm profit", specific promised percentages, "₹5,000 se ₹50,000"
- P&L screenshots, broker terminal profit screens, balance screenshots
- Stock names with price targets in an advertisement
- Testimonials referencing profit amounts
- Urgency framing implying financial loss from inaction

**Mandatory in every ad:**
- Name as registered with SEBI, SEBI registration number, registered office address, brand/trade name, CIN
- Declaration that information is accurate, true and complete
- Standard warning at ≥10pt: *"Investments in securities market are subject to market risks. Read all the related documents carefully before investing."*
- For registered entities: *"Registration granted by SEBI … does not guarantee performance or provide any assurance of returns to investors."*

Ads may require pre-approval through the RAASB route — Compliance Officer confirms the current process. Archive every creative **7 years** with approver name and timestamp. No paid association with unregistered finfluencers.

## 5. Platform verification (the account-block fix)

- Register firm contact details on the **SEBI SI Portal**; use the **exact same email and mobile** on Meta and Google.
- Meta: SEBI advertiser verification for securities ads (since 31 Jul 2025); pure-education ads may use the alternate business-document route — which is why brands have separate ad accounts.
- Google: India financial-services verification.
- Never evade enforcement (new accounts, borrowed BMs, cloaking) — it fails structurally and creates a regulatory record.

## 6. AI usage

- **Responsibility for research services sits solely with the RA, irrespective of AI usage.** AI use must be disclosed and registered.
- Hard rule: **no AI generates, approves or transmits a securities recommendation.** AI drafts; a registered analyst's sign-off (name + timestamp, logged) is a blocking gate before anything reaches a client.
- If signal delivery could touch broker execution or API order placement, get the algo-trading-framework question answered by the Compliance Officer **in writing** before scaling.

## 7. Records and clocks

- Retention: 5 years minimum for calls, agreements, KYC, research reports and rationale; **7 years for advertisements** with approval records.
- Call recordings disclosed to the caller; a statistically meaningful weekly sample audited against the QA rubric; breaches enter the register with remediation.
- Grievances: log with source (direct / SCORES), run the regulatory clock, escalate at 70% of clock. Mis-selling complaints go to Compliance immediately.
- Client data: dashboards and reports use **family/client codes only** — PII stays in the CRM under access controls.

## 8. Sales-floor language rules (what a closer may never say)

No return promises, no "guaranteed", no market-beating claims, no urgency-by-fear, no advice without registration context, no describing education products as advisory. The compliant answer to "kitna return milega?" is process, not numbers: we teach/research; markets carry risk; returns are never assured — in writing and on calls.

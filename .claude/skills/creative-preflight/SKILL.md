# Creative Pre-Flight — 5 Circles

Every creative passes all nine rules or it does not run. The approver's name and timestamp are recorded; the creative and its record are archived 7 years. Verdicts are per-rule, then overall.

## The nine rules

1. **No return/performance claim** — no "double", "sure-shot", "guaranteed", "risk-free", "confirm profit", no percentages promised, no "₹X se ₹Y".
2. **No P&L imagery** — no broker terminal screenshots, no profit screens, no balance shots (Zerodha/Groww or any other).
3. **No stock + target in an ad** — naming a stock with a price target is a recommendation in an advertisement.
4. **No profit testimonials** — testimonials must be genuine, consented, and silent on profit amounts.
5. **No loss-urgency** — nothing implying financial loss from not buying ("miss this and stay poor").
6. **Identity block present** — name as registered with SEBI, SEBI reg. no., registered office, brand name, CIN.
7. **Accuracy declaration present** — information is accurate, true and complete.
8. **Risk warning present at ≥10pt** — "Investments in securities market are subject to market risks. Read all the related documents carefully before investing."
9. **Right brand, right account** — education creative on the education brand/account; research creative on the RA brand/account under the full RA Ad Code. Never crossed.

## Worked examples

**FAIL — "Rakesh sir ne ₹10,000 ko 3 mahine me ₹85,000 banaya. Aap bhi seekho!"**
Breaks 1 (implied return), 4 (profit testimonial), 5 (aspirational-loss framing). Unfixable by editing the numbers out of the same claim — the angle itself is the violation.

**FAIL — screenshot of a green P&L with "Course lene ke baad ka result 📈".**
Breaks 2 outright; also 1 by implication. P&L imagery never passes, even blurred, even "illustrative".

**PASS — "Chart dekhna sabko aata hai. Padhna kisi kisi ko. 8 ghante ka recorded course, Hindi me, lifetime access — ₹1,999."**
Curiosity + process-credibility, one price, no promise. Passes 1–5; add the identity block, declaration and risk warning (6–8) in the landing/footer, run on the education account (9).

**PASS (research brand) — "Har call ke saath likha hua rationale. Analyst ka naam. SEBI-registered research. Yahi farak hai."**
Registration as differentiator, no performance claim. Full RA Ad Code block mandatory; research account only.

## Angles that work without breaking rules

Process-credibility ("we show our working") · founder/analyst authority · honest anti-tips positioning ("tips walon se bachiye") · curiosity about a real concept · social proof by volume ("10,000+ trained"), never by profit.

## Procedure

Draft → self-check against rules 1–5 → attach identity/declaration/warning block → route to pre-flight queue (OPERATOR M6) → approver ticks all nine → approve/reject with name + timestamp → live only from an approved state → performance (spend, leads, CPL) logged per creative → archive, never delete.
